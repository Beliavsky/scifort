Third-party license for the TFQMR Krylov recurrence in
src/sparse_linalg.f90

Fortran TFQMR adapts the existing SciFort BSD-3-Clause implementation in
src/optimize/krylov_iterative.f90, which follows the TFQMR recurrence in
SciPy's scipy/sparse/linalg/_isolve/tfqmr.py (v1.17.0):
https://github.com/scipy/scipy/blob/v1.17.0/scipy/sparse/linalg/_isolve/tfqmr.py
The new sparse solver specializes the algorithm to canonical one-based
real CSR matrices with mathematically consistent right preconditioning,
optional Jacobi or ILU(0), and true-residual convergence verification.
