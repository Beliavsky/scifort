Attribution for generalized Davenport-angle quaternion decomposition

The algorithm implemented in src/spatial_transform.f90 follows the
published Shuster-Markley and Bernardes-Viollet generalized Euler
formula, cross-checked and adapted from SciPy 1.17.0:
  scipy/spatial/transform/_rotation_xp.py
  https://github.com/scipy/scipy/blob/v1.17.0/scipy/spatial/transform/_rotation_xp.py
