Third-party attribution for the complex Golub--Kahan / MINRES recurrence in
src/sparse_linalg_complex_lsmr.f90.

This Fortran implementation adapts SciFort's existing real LSMR implementation,
scifort_optimize_lsmr (src/optimize/lsmr.f90), which adapts the SciPy LSMR
recurrence from scipy/sparse/linalg/_isolve/lsmr.py (SciPy 1.17.0):
https://github.com/scipy/scipy/blob/v1.17.0/scipy/sparse/linalg/_isolve/lsmr.py
The complex implementation uses the true Hermitian adjoint and SciFort CSR/CSC
structures. This BSD-3-Clause attribution is retained separately from SciFort's
MIT-licensed files.
