# Real QZ and ordered-block validation, 2026-11-15

Environment: Windows, GNU Fortran (GCC) **17.0.0 20260510 (experimental)**,
Python 3.13, SciPy **1.15.3**, binary64.

## Scope and backend provenance

Real-output overloads of `qz` and `ordqz` and real `ordqz_factors` support
orders 0..40. The existing complex overloads and original complex specific
procedure name remain intact. Real denominators use `real(dp)` and numerators
use `complex(dp)`. Sorting closes custom selections over whole conjugate
pairs; factor-only reordering is transactional on failure.

The numerical engine adapts Reference-LAPACK **v3.12.1** `DGGHRD`, `DHGEQZ`,
`DTGEXC`/`DTGEX2` and their private support closure. All 52 exact source files,
the selected safe-scaling definitions and the retained full license are listed
in the [source catalog](../docs/linalg_real_qz_sources.md). No external
LAPACK/BLAS package is linked. The preliminary QR and public range/status/
reconstruction checks are original SciFort code. Numerical kernel algorithms,
constants, exceptional shifts and block-swap acceptance tests are unchanged.

## Native tests

Both checked `-O0` and `-O3` builds pass **343 new native checks**:

- Real and conjugate eigenvalues, infinite eigenvalues with singular B,
  all-zero finite eigenvalues, all-infinite regular pencils, and empty input.
- Real reconstruction, orthogonality, quasi/upper-triangular structure and
  nonnegative second-factor diagonal under independent real basis changes.
- Half-plane and unit-circle sorting counts, pair-closed custom selection,
  ordered real factors and transactional rejection of malformed factors.
- Shape, optional output shape, unsupported real method, invalid sort,
  NaN/infinity, indeterminate pencil, and safely rejected large nonorthogonal
  bases. Fixtures avoid assigning an exact half-plane to a theoretically
  zero eigenvalue after an inexact basis change.

Existing native complex QZ, two-sided QZ, ordered QZ and generalized-eigenvalue
tests also pass with the new generic interfaces in both optimization profiles.

## Independent SciPy comparison

`validation/check_real_qz.py` uses seed **20261124** and 238 cases. Matrix
orders are 0/1/2/3/5/8/16/40, with dense random pairs, row-deficient B, diagonal
singular B and independent physical scales `A*1e150`, `B*1e-150`. Additional
orthogonally mixed constructed pencils contain real, conjugate and infinite
eigenvalues. Each pencil runs unsorted, four built-in sorts, a custom predicate
selecting positive imaginary alpha, and standalone factor reordering.

Checks cover independently scaled reconstruction, both orthogonal bases,
block structure including diagonal positive B blocks for conjugate pairs,
homogeneous eigenvalue sets with assignment matching, selected spectra and
pair-closed selection counts. Spectral comparisons use SciPy `eigvals` on
independently scaled pairs to avoid quotient overflow. SciPy `ordqz` is also
attempted in physical units; **16 reordering rejections** on the extreme-scale
fixtures are recorded rather than treated as successful references. All
native cases still require the independent reconstruction, complete spectrum,
selected spectrum and selection-count checks. No identical-factor or ordering
assumption is imposed on nonunique Schur decompositions.

| Metric | Checked -O0 | Checked -O3 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Successful real QZ/order/factor cases | 238 | 238 |
| Ordered spectra checked (nonempty) | 180 | 180 |
| Conjugate blocks checked | 735 | 735 |
| Maximum scaled reconstruction error | 4.88498e-15 | 4.83641e-15 |
| Maximum orthogonality error | 5.9952e-15 | 5.9952e-15 |
| Maximum homogeneous SciPy spectral error | 2.86438e-14 | 2.86438e-14 |

Tolerances are `3e-11` for scaled reconstruction, `3e-12` for orthogonality,
and `3e-8` for homogeneous spectral matching; structural zeros are checked
exactly, with a separate `3e-12` normalized allowance on the upper off-diagonal
entry of a conjugate B block. These are fixture-specific acceptance bounds,
not general forward accuracy guarantees for defective pencils.

## Unchanged upstream comparison

A separate ignored build compiles the **unchanged pinned source files**,
original `LSAME`/`XERBLA`, `LA_CONSTANTS` and `LA_XISNAN`, with checked `-O0`.
An interface-only module exposes those external routines to the same original
SciFort wrappers; the numerical upstream source is not patched or generated
from adapted kernels. Its driver independently passes all 238 SciPy checks.
This validates adaptation equivalence under the same wrapper/QR pipeline;
it does not independently validate those wrappers by itself.

For checked debug output, every printed numeric field is identical to this
separately compiled reference. Optimized factors can differ in sign and order,
so raw field differences are diagnostic only. Complete homogeneous spectra
agree with unchanged upstream to **4.21885e-15** at `-O3`; status and selected
counts agree in all fixtures. Independent reconstruction and selected spectra
are still required. The complete upstream LAPACK test suite was not run.

## Limits

The public order-40 limit remains. Real `method='inverse'`, batching,
condition estimates and C ABI expansion are outside this change. Near-defective
or irregular pencils can still be rejected or exhibit inaccurate forward
eigenvalues despite small backward errors. Real and complex selectors have
documented different treatments of numerically tiny nonzero beta. No global
mutable state or new linked library dependency is introduced.
