## Native checks

`test/test_interpolate_aaa.f90` passed **170 assertions** in both checked
`-O0` and optimized `-O3` builds. Coverage includes:

- Real rational values, support interpolation, scalar/vector agreement, and
  known complex poles, zeros, and residues; near-pole evaluation.
- Exponential approximation, support-budget exhaustion, and error diagnostics.
- Complex sample domains, complex values on real coordinates, and real values
  on complex coordinates; complex queries on real fits.
- Single-sample constants, zero functions, duplicate samples, nonfinite-value
  filtering, invalid coordinates/options/shapes, and uninitialized objects.
- Coordinate scales from subnormal `1.3e-320` through `1e100`, value scales
  through `1e308`, coordinate offsets, and physical residue rescaling.
- Guarded rejection of unrepresentable Loewner quotients with traps enabled,
  and overflow-free unscaling of subnormal Loewner columns.
- Exact discrete interpolation with more than 39 support points; explicit
  spectral and automatic/manual cleanup size diagnostics with retained fits.
- Weak-pole removal, post-cleanup sample error/convergence, invalid cleanup,
  and preservation of the representation when cleanup fails.

## Independent comparisons

`validation/check_aaa.py` and `validation/aaa_reference_driver.f90` passed
**34 cases, 4,114 query values, and 55 analytic spectral values** per build.
Cases use 201 samples and 121 query points each, with tolerance variation
`1e-8`/`1e-11`, real and complex domains, rational functions, exponentials,
hyperbolic tangents, sines, logarithms, gamma functions, deterministic noisy
data, and weak-pole cleanup with multiple thresholds.

No reference case reported an unexpected cleanup or spectral failure. Exact
rational pole/zero sets are matched by minimum-distance assignment rather
than eigenvalue ordering; residues are paired with the corresponding poles.
Their normalized location errors are bounded by `1e-8`, with residue absolute
and relative tolerances `1e-8`. The weak-pole fixture uses location tolerance
`1e-6` after refitting perturbed data.

General function comparisons use
`8*max(rtol,epsilon**0.75)*(max(abs(samples))+abs(reference))`, reflecting AAA's
global sample-norm criterion and possible support differences between SVD
backends. Strong cleanup uses `1e-7*(1+abs(reference))`; the noisy case uses
`1e-5*(1+abs(reference))` because fits below the noise floor can take different
paths. The maximum observed error divided by its applicable bound was
approximately **0.06751** in both configurations. Native exact-function tests
use tighter function-specific bounds, including a relative `1e-8` check
close to a known pole.

These comparisons validate the tested approximations, not equality of
support-point choices or the numerical rank selected by different backends.
SciPy warnings are suppressed only in the comparison harness, where iteration
limits and aggressive cleanup are intentional cases. The native API reports
convergence and cleanup conditions through explicit statuses.

## Limits

The native generalized-eigenvalue backend permits order-40 pencils, so spectral
methods and cleanup are limited to 39 retained support points. Fitting and
evaluation remain available beyond that limit. Close/repeated poles, severe
degree cancellation, roots numerically close to infinity, and noisy/undersampled
data require care. Sample convergence is not a bound on extrapolation error.
Only the listed Windows/GNU compiler environment was tested; C ABI and wrapper
expansion are deferred. See the [API guide](../docs/interpolate_aaa.md).
